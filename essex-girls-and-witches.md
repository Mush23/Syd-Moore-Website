## The Essex Girls Liberation Front

Syd co-founded the Essex Girls Liberation Front in 2017 to challenge the "Essex girl" stereotype. The campaign ran the exhibition *This is What an Essex Girl Looks Like* at the Beecroft Art Gallery in Southend (2019–20). In December 2020 it persuaded Oxford University Press to remove the term from the *Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary*.

[Follow the EGLF on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/WEARETHEEGLF)

## Walk with the accused

Syd led the creative-writing workshops for Manningtree's twelve-stop *Revisiting the Essex Witch Trials* trail, and wrote and recorded the Basildon "Witch" Walk, which remembers nine women accused between 1574 and 1653. Both were part of the University of Essex's Snapping the Stiletto project.

- [The Manningtree trail](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/snapping-the-stiletto/manningtree)
- [The Basildon "Witch" Walk](https://www.essex.ac.uk/research-projects/snapping-the-stiletto/basildon-witch-walk)
