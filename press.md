## Fact sheet

- **Books:** the Section W trilogy (Magpie/Oneworld), the Essex Witch Museum Mysteries (Point Blank/Oneworld), and the standalone novels *The Witching Hour* and *Witch Hunt* (Avon/HarperCollins).
- **Shortlists:** Dead Good Reader Awards 2018, Holmes & Watson Award (Rosie Strange and Sam Stone); CWA Short Story Dagger 2019 ("Death Becomes Her") and 2020 ("Easily Made").
- **Residencies:** Essex Book Festival Writer in Residence 2018; first Author in Residence for Essex Libraries, 2023.
- **Television:** presenter of Channel 4's books programme *Pulp*.
- **Campaigning:** co-founder of the Essex Girls Liberation Front (2017).
- **Writing community:** Royal Literary Fund Writing for Life Fellow; leads the RLF Reading Round in hospices.
