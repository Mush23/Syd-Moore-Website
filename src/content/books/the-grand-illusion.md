In June 1940, Daphne Devine assists the stage magician Jonty Trevelyon, until the secret service comes calling. The pair are drafted into a hidden unit of occultists and surrealists beneath Wormwood Scrubs. Their mission is to turn Hitler's obsession with the occult into a weapon against him.

Readers of the Essex Witch Museum Mysteries will spot a familiar face: Septimus makes an appearance.
