The third Section W novel, *The Final Act of Daphne Devine*, is published in paperback and ebook on 8 October 2026.

Posing as rare-book dealers, Daphne and Jack slip back into enemy territory. They carry an ancient grimoire meant to shake the beliefs of an SS officer, but betrayal is closer than they think.

New to the series? Start with [The Grand Illusion](/books/the-grand-illusion), then [The Great Deception](/books/the-great-deception).
