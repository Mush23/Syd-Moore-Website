This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we've done

- Text and background colours meet AA contrast ratios.
- Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- Book covers have text alternatives, and headings are in a logical order.
- Pages respect your device's "reduce motion" setting, and nothing plays automatically.
- Every page is tested with automated accessibility checks before it is published.

## Known issues

Some links go to retailer and festival websites that we don't control, and those sites may not meet the same standard.

## Report a problem

If something on this site doesn't work for you, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact). Say which page you were on and what happened, and we'll aim to reply within 10 working days.
